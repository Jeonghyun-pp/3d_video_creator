## Decide yourself; ask only these
Why: on 2026-10-07 nothing said what was yours to decide, so undefined choices fell to the safest minimum (a prism for a cast head) or to a question nobody could answer.
**Ask the user only for**: paid calls (above), approvals and picks in their words (#3) - the story direction (`story pick`) and the shots to redo after the clay reel (`reel clay`) included, edits to frozen code, a large deviation on a specific real subject (#4), and anything outside the request (publishing, purchases, other projects). Everything else, decide yourself.
Yours to decide - then record it (`decide note --topic --choice --why`) and carry on: modelling method and part breakdown, detail above the floor (#8), camera candidates and the pick (in a delegated run: a takes sheet a watcher judged - `storyboard judge`, then `storyboard pick --agent-note`; on the ladder the user's words), how many previews, subagents and their effort, contrib entries, retry strategy, which frames to spend counted renders on.
Bad: "Should I model the runners as sweeps or ask first?" Good: "Runners: sweep with path_smooth catmull_rom and a 1.0→0.7 taper - smooth bends like the photo (decision note #7)."
A delegated run (`project.delegation`, the user's words recorded by `project delegate` or the launcher's `--delegate`) skips the decision-ladder sheets entirely: the request's decisions are the user's, the rest are yours under this section.

**When the studio asks you to read or paint** (`OUTSIDE_MODEL_ASKED`, 2026-10-18): where your sandbox denies the network the studio cannot start its reader or painter, so it writes a request file and stops. Hand the request file - and nothing else - to a fresh subagent (same model as you; for a paint, one with an image tool), take its answer, then `studio ask answer --request <file> --answer <its JSON or image> --model <its model id> --by "a fresh subagent"` and run the command again (`studio ask pending --project <p>` lists the open ones). Why: the reader must not know what the picture was meant to show - one that does reads the intent, not the picture (the reason the studio used a separate reader); measured, a fresh agent of the same model gave the same 55 answers as the studio's reader. Never answer it yourself, never type a reading by hand to get past it. Bad: you drew the beat picture, then answer its request from memory of the prompt. Good: a new subagent given only `<project>/asks/ab12…/request.json` opens the picture paths it names and returns the JSON. A command that asks several questions (a video review, frames proposals) lists every request at once: answer them all, then run it once. Details: `references/visual_review.md`.

## Non-Negotiable Rules
Violations here are what made past reels wrong or cost money. Measurements behind each: `references/rule_rationale.md`.

<CRITICAL>
**#1 — Each cut's maker follows what must be right in it, generative first**: every shot-list cut describes its screen before any picture - `frame {focus: [ids], size: wide|medium|close|detail}` with its `show` and `direction` (`FRAME_UNDESCRIBED` otherwise). What must be right in a cut is what its lines say on screen, nothing more (the user, 2026-10-20: "대사가 말하는것만"): a working thing a line explains, on screen, keeps its shape; a number said of a solid thing on screen, its count; the key change of what the lines speak of, its motion (`maker.implied_keeps`). Never write `keeps` (`KEEPS_NOT_READ`): when planners wrote them, 273 on 187 cuts sent a third of the reel to Blender. To make something exact, say it in a line the cut shows. `library/production_methods.json` says what each method keeps (generative: a look held by a shared start picture, nothing exact; hybrid: the solid things put back from Blender pixels after the take; Blender: everything) and `maker.choose` takes the method that keeps all of it and leaves the most of the frame to a model; a model that would repaint under a quarter of the frame is no better than Blender alone. Why: the user, 2026-10-20: "엄청 상세한 부품정도만 blender 로 하고, 생성모델을 많이 쓰는게", then "원리 컷/세상 컷 이렇게 이분법적으로 나누는것도 좀 그렇지않나" - the same building is the world in one cut and the mechanism in the next; what decides is what the viewer must be able to trust. Bad: every cut showing the subject marked hybrid, or a lock close-up left generative while its line says how the valve opens. Good: the typhoon tower from afar, its line about the storm → generative; the tower cut open, its line saying how the ball hangs → hybrid, the ball put back from Blender; the ball's lag as the key change filling the frame (`detail`) → Blender. A thing several generated cuts hold in focus keeps one look from a shared picture (`concept brief` attaches the first such cut's start picture; Veo reads only the first frame). The user changes a cut by `maker {mode, why}`; never with a method that drops a keep (`MAKER_KEEP_UNCOVERED`). The hybrid's look is not yet measured: a paid check with the user's cap comes before trusting it. A generated cut is built from its start picture (image to video), skips the camera solve, sits in the clay as its picture until made, and its motion is passed by a person (`video-review`). Within that: `route plan` from `route_features`, never from how the goal text sounds; ask the user on R0. Exact geometry, motion, identity and anchored labels stay in Blender; generation owns looks, phenomena and atmosphere. Set `route.role`: `explain` = Blender, or a hybrid take whose declared key parts held or were put back from Blender (labels ride on kept parts or measured anchors); `mood` = generated, captions only. Why: all eleven whole-frame restyles looked photoreal and failed whole-frame edges (IoU 0.07–0.23), so an explain take is judged on the parts the narration names, not on every edge. → `references/routing.md`, `references/generative_safety.md`
**#2 — Words and marks never live in generated or lit pixels**: every generation prompt says "no text, no letters, no captions" and holds no quoted strings; titles are 2D `shot.titles` inside the safe rect, arrows/dimensions/outlines are `shot.graphics` on their own layer, labels follow 3D anchors or measured 2D anchors. Why: generated video garbles letters; 3D text gets lit, bloomed and leaks into control passes (s01's 3D title sat 78 % outside the frame). Instead of 3D text or mesh arrows, use those layers; words that belong on a surface (a station sign, a tag on a column) are `shot.scene.decals` - drawn in the project font as an image on a lit card, role `decal`, out of every control pass and kept from Blender in a generated take. Quantities on screen: a title `count` ('{n}' counts from -> to), a title `box` (callout panel), `shot.charts` (bars that grow in). → `references/titles.md`, `references/explainer_graphics.md`
**#3 — Decisions come only from the user's words, and their words reach every value**: approvals, budgets, fill-brief sign-off, take picks and repair resets are recorded with the user's own words, verbatim; a candidate is never an approved master. A run started without its user (the launcher: `STUDIO_UNATTENDED`) records no user's words at all - every `--user-words` is refused (`USER_WORDS_UNATTENDED`), even a quote from its request: stop and write in REPORT.md what the user is to decide, with your recommendation (2026-10-18: "do as recommended" was recorded as the pick of a shot whose takes the user had turned down). Any content value of a shot (`project.SHOT_FIELD_LAYER` content: camera, scene, actions, titles, graphics, labels, render, key and concealed parts, screen) or a fill brief changes when the user asks: a word-op if one fits, otherwise `set`/`add`/`remove` on its path (`storyboard revise --ops`, `fill revise --ops`, workbench `set_shot_value`) - not a lesser fallback; a value nothing reads is refused and the error lists what is read - pick from it. Why: the gates trust those records; on 2026-10-06 words reached only distance/height/angle, so slide `span` and macro_push `detail_fill` were out of reach, and crane/dive_through "higher" named params the moves never read - it built and changed nothing. Instead of writing or paraphrasing an approval, show the sheet and ask; instead of "that can't be changed", `set` it and say what changed (`path: before → after`). → `references/storyboard.md`
**#4 — Real things are sourced; invented things say so**: a specific real subject takes its numbers from ≥ 2 sources, `subject trace` (drawing pixels) or `subject fit`, never from numbers typed into a script; a deliberate change is a spec `deviation` with the user's words for large changes; every narration sentence rests on a sourced claim or is marked illustrative without numbers (`facts check`). Why: LLM-picked numbers made the toy jet and the 0.62 m drawing offset; unsourced claims about a real station reached a public repo. → `references/subject_fidelity.md`
**#5 — Fix the work, never the check**: when a gate fails — in order: 1) re-trace or add stations where the silhouette diverges, 2) widen `free` bounds or add parameters, 3) split the part, 4) a better reference (CAD, second view), 5) ask the user. Forbidden: lowering tolerances or `min_iou`, deleting a claim or check, editing a sourced number to pass. Each rebuild names one `--diagnosis`; the engine keeps the best passing version. → `references/workbench.md`
**#6 — Broken output is a build error, not a look**: real scale before look (scale audit), no person through a thing (`CHARACTER_THROUGH_OBJECT`), shells/fog/markings tagged with scene roles before any look or control pass, cameras that travel are `camera.move`/`camera.rig` (guards: no pass through geometry, clip, pitch), reveals cut closed outward meshes, falling matter is a baked `simulate` action. Why: an untagged 400 m earth box set the lighting bounds (lamps at +64 m); hand keys flew through walls; a live simulation renders differently per worker. → `references/scene_roles.md`, `references/camera_rig.md`, `references/scatter_simulation.md`
**#7 — Free in Blender, never around the build**: an author script may use any bpy; new geometry kinds, profiles and joint laws go in `contrib/<kind>/<name>/` (pure function + manifest, `contrib:<name>@draft`, promoted after a passing build); looks go in `shot.render.{grade, compositor, engine_settings, addons}`. The build itself refuses what reaches around it (processes, network, dynamic code, handlers, files outside the build, frozen code: `AUTHOR_SCRIPT_REFUSED`, `AUTHOR_SANDBOX_VIOLATION`, `FROZEN_CODE_CHANGED`) - when refused, change the data the check reads or add a contrib entry. Why: the author used to share the gates' interpreter and could replace a gate unnoticed. `CONTRIB_NEEDS_BROKER` (inside your sandbox no entry can be checked): call `studio_run` with `["contrib", "check", "--project", P, "--name", N]`, then run the command again - never turn a sandbox off or check an entry by hand to get past it. Bad: "contrib check crashed in the sandbox, so I'll build without it." Good: "studio_run contrib check → ok, verdict kept → shot build via studio_run." → `references/blender_freedom.md`
**#8 — Build what shows, concretely**: before modelling, list the reference's detail in four tiers - silhouette, major forms (block, head, runners), secondary (ribs, bosses, flanges, bolts, clamps), finish (fillets, chamfers, cast surface, parting lines) - and model every item, or write it in the simplification table (item, max on-screen px, reason). The only valid reason to simplify is size on screen: below the feature's `min_screen_px` (default 24 px); "no time" or "looks enough" is not one. A visible part that is a coarse primitive fails the fidelity `detail` check (on-screen px per distinct face direction > 40: plain slabs, prisms, faceted tubes) unless its builder says `plain: "<why the real part is plain>"`; a contrib parameter the output ignores fails `CONTRIB_PARAM_UNUSED`. Forbidden: raising segment counts or subdividing to pass the check - that adds faces, not detail; instead model the missing tier (ribs, bosses, vents, grooves) or declare `plain` with the reason. Why: in the engine test the head and block were one octagonal prism whose `rib` parameter did nothing, while the mechanism inside matched to 1e-8 m. Bad: "cylinder head: contrib ported_cast (prism), detail later". Good: "head: `casting` - body + 4 cam-journal bosses + 6 ribs + 4 plug bosses, `fillet_m` 0.004, `cuts` for the deck face and plug bores; omitted: casting numbers (6 px on screen)". → `references/subject_fidelity.md`
**#9 — The narration is true; the pictures may cheat, declared**: what a line's claims speak of (their `about`), a spec subject's parts and places, and a state are never cheated; the lens, the angle, a neighbour made see-through, a smaller plume, a stand-in moved, scaled or turned, the subject turned on its own axis are, as `cheat_core.CHEATS` rows within their limits. A picture that cannot be shot: `frames check` first (the cheapest cheat that keeps its punch), a redraw second - never a quietly duller view (`FRAME_FLATTENED`). A moment no cheat solves comes back redraw with proposals - A: a picture redrawn from an angle outside its intent, B: a must-see part dropped (never one a line explains that no other moment shows) - shown by `frames proposals` and taken only on the user's pick (`frames pick` / `frames drop`), never by the engine: an angle outside the intent changes the picture, which is the user's. When the picture camera fails, in order: 1) `frames check` (its cheats - a thing round the subject faded is `see_context`; water and glass the world declares `medium` never block); 2) its A/B proposals to the user; last: hand keys, built only with `camera.off_picture_why` after `frames check` ran (`CAMERA_OFF_PICTURE` stops the build otherwise; the clay sheet lists every such shot). Forbidden: searching your own views for one that shows every part - it backs off to one far, high diagonal for every shot (2026-10-20, canal: five shots one picture, `PICTURE_REPEATS`). Why: the user, 2026-10-14: "이건 논문이 아니라 사람들한테 보여줄 영상인만큼 실제를 고집하다 영상이 노잼될 우려"; angle and lens left to the agent in the user's words, recorded in `project.policy.viewpoint_cheat`. Bad: turning a hero low angle to eye level to clear a pillar. Good: `context_move pillar 0.6 m` recorded with the picture. → `references/frames.md`
**#10 — A gate is closed until its inputs exist; what passes is what was made, and only the user loosens a check** (2026-10-21; the submarine clay was grey bars and every check passed):
1) a missing input fails, never skips - every layer an operation stands on is settled on the ladder or off it (`decide write`; `DECISION_MISSING`), a layer is linted against parents that exist (`PARENT_MISSING`), every claim says its `about`, a generated clip is passed by a person on every project, an asset with no manifest is `ASSET_UNKNOWN`, a pictured moment with no current reading is `FRAME_PICTURE_UNREAD`, a reel flow that measured no shot is `REEL_FLOW_UNMEASURED`;
2) judge what was made, not its name - the world's subject is judged by its measured spec (`SUBJECT_UNMEASURED`: a box named like it is not it; one placed by `scene.instances` is measured too), built from photos of its kind first (`PHOTOS_MISSING`; only the user's words exempt one: `subject no-photo`), a take's structure is held only by declared parts measured and put back, a candidate is cut from final renders, a review counts only as `review record` wrote it with the user's words;
3) a value that weakens a check - a looser strictness, a blocking gate set to warn, a hybrid cut's `mood` over a Blender look, a world subject's non-real `subject_mode`, the photo attach-consent - stands only with the user's words for that very value (`WEAKENING_UNAPPROVED`; `project weaken --what ... --user-words`); a delegated run's launch words never count;
4) what was not measured is said (`not_measured`), and refused when nothing was. Bad: "no spec yet, so the fidelity check has nothing to say - build". Good: "PHOTOS_MISSING - reference collect for the hull, then the spec, then the build".
</CRITICAL>

## Craft defaults (follow them; override with a one-line reason in the change or commit)
- Render on GPU (`STUDIO_RENDER_DEVICE=GPU`, Metal ~11× CPU); CPU, one worker, one small frame first only when the user limits load.
- Genre styles: `style learn-genre --genre <name> --folder <local reels>` learns motion, look and composition from every reel of one genre under one name (target 10 or more; fewer is warned `GENRE_FEW_REFERENCES`); use it as `camera.move.style` / `render.look_style`. A style learned from one reel copies that reel.
- Camera: choose the move from what the narration shows (`dive_through`, `pass_between`, `descend_levels`, `section_push`, `waypoints`; one object: `turntable`, `slide`, `macro_push`), timing from a motion style + `camera fit --apply` (a declared `move.timing.profile` other than burst_settle - a constant push, a slow retreat - is kept and only scored; `--profile burst_settle` asks for the style's candidate); for hero or high-energy shots compare 2–3 variants in the workbench. → `references/camera_rig.md`
- Shots follow the camera, not the sentences: one unbroken camera is one shot (several sentences, each with its own subtitle time), built in one scene that holds every space it passes (`scene.use` + `instances`). Imitating a reference: `reference cuts` first, cut only where it cuts (`REFERENCE_CUT_MISMATCH` refuses builds); `examples/samsung_cutaway` shot lengths are sentence lengths, not cuts. A plain cut from fast motion into stillness is a jolt (`CUT_MOTION_JUMP` in `qa collect`); a held frame cut into a burst is the genre's grammar. Why: archcut3 (2026-10-08) cut a 6 s unbroken dive at 3.1 s into a separately built hall - the view jumped. Instead of splitting at a sentence, carry the camera through; follow the reference shot's own rhythm with `camera fit --reference <video> --range a:b`. → `references/camera_rig.md` (One camera, one shot)
- Building elements, context and repeated background come from exemplars, `env_kits.street` and `scatter`, not bare boxes; lit windows are the `window_grid` shader. → `references/building_elements.md`, `references/environment_kits.md`
- Readable frame: hold the horizon where the style learned it (~0.40 for cutaway aerials), keep sky (`street(..., sightline=...)`), show structure as a section (`section_push` + `section.stage`). → `references/section_staging.md`
- What fills a level is decided by topic with the user: `fill propose` → their words → `fill revise` → `fill approve`. → `references/fill_brief.md`
- A place around an exact subject comes in this order: the place in words (brief `place`) → the view (storyboard camera) → the relation (`shot.scene.backdrop.relation`: support, view, light) and the backdrop objects → the backdrop image (`generate backdrop-review --shot`, made for that view) → build. Why: on robot_joint, factory images pasted behind exact gears looked like the gears floated (no support, studio light against a bright hall); with the relation the gears stood on the bench and the hall showed past its edge. Instead of pasting a backdrop after the fact, decide the relation and let the scene and the prompt both follow it. → `references/backdrop.md`
- Taste gates warn, broken output blocks: frame-probe size/edge, framing, subject margin, arrow legibility, fill limits, shape on schematic subjects, depth of field/shake/two-point and `detail_placeholder` are warnings by default (`studio/gates.py` kind `taste`); a covered subject, an empty level, wrong real scale (`broken`) and the repair budget (`budget`) block. Read every warning in the build result and fix the ones that show in the picture, inside the request's appearance budget; do not rebuild to silence one. Why: on 2026-10-07 every discarded build of the engine run was a taste gate (a key part plainly visible at 0.17 % of the frame) while the two real defects were seen by eye. A project may tighten (`policy.gates: {CODE: error}`, `strictness: all-strict`) or loosen (`look-first`); nothing else can be softened.

## Phase D — Decide with the user (before anything expensive)
**CRITICAL — the plan is a story picked from different directions, and the user spends three moments** (2026-10-17; the rocket's 25 shots passed every check and the user called the clay reel undeliverable: one upright cylinder mid-frame in 20 shots, no place, a camera that never rested, 3.95 s shots; and "기획단계에서 너무 시간 안쓰고싶어"). The user's time: ① the request; ② one of three story directions (pitches first: `story pitch-brief` → your own lenses from the facts (`pitch-lenses`) → 2-4 pitches per lens, each lens in its own pass (`pitch-add`) → group the alike (`pitch-group`) → a blind tournament (`pitch-judge-pack`, 3 judges, `pitch-judged`) → `story directions` makes the best pitches of three groups the directions - never a draw of kinds (2026-10-20: the draw gave ten topics the same three directions); write each from its pitch, prose first, then the body → `story add` → judged blind against the reference reels before the user sees them: `story judge-pack` → three judges who know nothing of which is ours → `story judged`; a direction that loses most of its pairs (`STORY_LOSES`, with the judges' reasons) is rewritten once from them and judged again, never twice → `story sheet` shows the winners and folds the rest → `story pick` in their words, any of them); ③ the clay reel (`reel clay`: every built shot captioned, one page with the reel flow) and the shots they name (`reel redo start/add/build/pick`). Everything between is yours (delegated) or one sheet each (on the ladder). The story (`story_core.STORY_RULES`): a hook first and over in 3 s, a turn or climax, the climax at 0.55-0.9, tension that moves; the script's lines name their beat, shots follow the beats (`STORY_*`). **The beats are a chain, not a list** (2026-10-19; the bike reel read as "부품만 쭉 보여주는" - lever, and pulley, and chain, and cage; the study set's reels cannot be reordered): the story names its `thread` (`kind` from `library/story_axes.json` thread - moving, time, question, change, failure, scale - and the `carrier_ids` that keep it on screen), every beat after the hook says its `link` - `so` (because of the last), `but` (against it: a doubt, a complication, what goes wrong), `and` (only to name the next stop) - and the questions run through: `raises` on a beat, `answers: [beat ids]` on the ones that close them. Errors: `STORY_THREAD_MISSING`, `STORY_LINK_MISSING`, `STORY_LIST` (over 3 'and's in a row or over 0.6 of the links - past the study set), `HOOK_UNANSWERED`; warnings: `HOOK_ANSWERED_EARLY` (before 0.3), `QUESTION_DANGLING`, `STORY_CLOSE_OPEN` (9 of 10 study reels end back at the opening), `STORY_NO_BUT` (one per 30 s). A story approved before stands (`STORY_CHAIN_UNSET` warns); a re-plan writes them. **The viewer sees it happen and has something at stake** (2026-10-20; ten topics planned blind won 6 of 10 pairs against the study set, and all four losses were judged "a tour of the mechanism, nothing at stake" - every winner opened on something happening): the hook is an event on screen - `on_screen {event, kind: contradiction|failure|payoff_first|anomaly}`, never a question asked (`HOOK_UNSEEN`, an error; its question goes in `raises`); at least one `but` beat goes wrong on screen (`on_screen`, kind failure or change - `TURN_SPOKEN_ONLY`); no more than 4 beats in a row that neither raise nor answer (`STORY_TOUR`; the study set at most 5, 8 of 10 at most 4); the story names `stake {who, what_is_at_risk, closed_by}` - its pitch's own, never one fitted from a list - and closes on it - `closed_by` the last beat (`STAKE_MISSING`, `STAKE_NOT_CLOSED`); the last beat `returns_to` the hook, its picture again, now understood (`CLOSE_NOT_RETURNING`). A story settled before these stands (`STORY_STAKE_UNSET` warns). Bad hook: "have you ever wondered how a lift knows where to stop?" (a line). Good: "the lift doors open on a bare shaft wall - it stopped between floors" (`on_screen` anomaly; raises: why did it stop there?). Bad: "the lever moves; the pulley moves; the chain moves; the cage turns" (any order would do). Good: "press the lever - **but** nothing shifts (why?) - **so** look: the pulley moved, **but** the chain sits still - **so** turn the crank - the chain follows the pulley up the next sprocket (answers the hook)". The pictures (`reel_flow.REEL_FLOW`, the study set measured): a new place opens wide, a wide shot comes back, no two neighbours the same picture, 30 % or more of the shots at rest (the climax aside), a median shot under 3 s, the climax cut faster - and the 15 direction rules below. Options come from width, then a blind choice, never one ask for "something creative": a model asked once returns one safe option several times, so story pitches are written lens by lens in separate passes and judged blind; pictures are drawn on axes (`concept.CONCEPT_AXES` - one near the user's taste, one far from it, one never tried). No direction fits: their mix (`story pick --body`), else a new pitch round - never a fourth draft of the same pitch. The story pick is never delegated: a delegated run stops at the story sheet and waits for the user's number and why. Bad: three story drafts that each open on the subject and explain in order, or the same three kinds for every topic. Good (three pitch groups of one topic, a lift): the doors open on a bare shaft wall between floors; a full car and an empty one stop level with the same floor; the brake catches a car that was let fall on purpose in a test. → `references/story.md`
Delegated run (`project.delegation` recorded): skip this phase - no sheets; settle every layer you write, in ladder order (brief → facts → the user's `story pick` → script → world → shotlist → look), with `decide write --layer <layer> --body <file> --agent-note "<why>"` (never `--user-words`: the story alone is the user's, through `story pick`), and record your other choices with `decide note`. Why (2026-10-18): a layer kept only in a file of your own, or only in world.json, is invisible to the tools after it - the beat reader, concept, frames and facts read `decisions/<layer>.json` - and the run stopped at its first beat picture. The user's taste (`pattern show` → `taste`, from their own words: `pattern taste add` after reading it back to them) sits beside the patterns: where they disagree, the taste wins unless the shot says why. Bad: copying the median `height_share` into `screen.targets` for every shot. Good: "explain: subject large and the camera held, as in the examples - height_share 0.7 in our square frame; leaves camera_speed: the push through the wall is the point of this shot". The storyboard sheet and the build compare the shot with its role (`SCREEN_PATTERN_OFF`, advice; only strong items count); a declared departure is never reported. → `references/screen_grammar.md` section 10

## Phase 0 — State and route
1. `doctor`, `project status`; resume useful results rather than restarting.
2. Inspect reference frames; write what must read in each shot, and when, before authoring.
3. `route plan` → show the user `route_plan.md` (route, reason, cost, minutes per shot). Paid shots wait for approval (HARD-GATE).

Bad: "Shot 3 shows the DDP facade at dusk, so generate it." Good: "Shot 3 features: exact_geometry (panel joints), photoreal_beyond_assets → R2 hybrid: Blender motion pass + video-to-video restyle; $3.3 estimate; awaiting approval."
Bad: routing a mechanism close-up to Veo because the reference reel used Veo. Good: mechanism → blender (R3); the reference's look is matched with a photoreal look preset, not by giving up exact motion.
Never decide a new case by matching the keywords of the closest example; apply the feature definitions in `references/routing.md`.

## Phase 1 — Assets
Order: local library → Poly Haven / ambientCG (CC0, studio-cleared) → CAD factory (standard dimensions) → image-to-3D (paid, review-only) → hand modelling. Details: `references/asset_ladder.md`.
Forbidden: marking a caller-supplied file `cleared`, using Hunyuan 3D (licence excludes Korea), or using an image-to-3D mesh's invented back side in a close-up without turnaround approval.

## Phase 1b — Subject spec (before any modelling)
The subject is never a clay box, at any stage, clay review included: `scene.stand_ins` refuses it (`STAND_IN_NOT_ALLOWED`) and a build that shows it with no measured subject spec fails (`SUBJECT_UNMEASURED`: an object carrying its name is not it). Photos (`subject_fidelity.md` 1b) come before the spec. Why: submarine 2026-10-21 skipped both on "a clay build needs no spec" and the reel was grey bars.
Bad: "WWII-style fighter" → six boxes and a slab wing with hand-typed sizes. Good: `subjects/p51d/spec.json` — identity P-51D Mustang, length 9.83 m and span 11.28 m from two sources, 11 fuselage stations, NACA wing with real taper and dihedral, belly radiator scoop, four-blade propeller, top-view silhouette registered by a datum, IoU ≥ 0.85 (auto-fitted: 0.968).
1. `subject init` → parts, builders, relations, `assembly_claims`; rough numbers allowed only as a start, with `free` bounds you can justify without the answer.
2. Drawing: silhouette with `px_per_m` and a `register` rule (a part face ↔ the drawing's extreme pixel, symmetry line ↔ axis 0) → `subject trace --register --part ...` → `subject fit` → read `candidates/*.json`, then `--apply`. CAD: `subject from-dxf`.
3. Building elements: `profile` (KS/EN table sections, exact polygons), `wall` (openings), `array pattern: grid`; set `dim_role` from `look_data/real_dimensions.json`. → `references/building_elements.md`
When fidelity fails — follow #5. Forbidden: lowering tolerances/`min_iou`, deleting a feature or claim, or marking a sourced number `assumed` to pass.

## Phase 2 — Author and build
- Blender commands run through the MCP tool `studio_run` (the CLI in your sandbox answers `BLENDER_NEEDS_BROKER`). Outside your sandbox only the sealed engine runs (2026-10-18): the user's approved copy of the studio (`studio seal status`); your edits to studio/ work in your own CLI at once and reach studio_run, the workbench and builds only when the user records them in their words (`studio freeze record`) - `ENGINE_SEAL_BEHIND` on a result says the checkout has moved on. Never try to make the outside run the checkout's code (a server added to .codex/config.toml is never read: your run starts elsewhere); ask the user to record the change instead. Bad: editing studio/blender.py and expecting the next studio_run build to use it. Good: "the fix is in studio/blender.py; it reaches builds once you record it (studio freeze record)." Explore in the workbench before writing code (`studio_workbench` MCP tools, or `workbench start/call/commit`): typed tools, millisecond measurements, ID previews where each part has its own colour and pixel count, `variant_save`/`compare`. `exec` (free bpy, `--allow-exec`) is for exploring: a session that ran it (even one that failed) cannot be committed - move what works into spec `ops`, a kit builder or typed tools, then commit a clean session. Commit replays the session through `shot build --base` and fails on any measurement difference. → `references/workbench.md`
- Spawning a subagent: pass `model` and `reasoning_effort` per task (table in `references/blender_freedom.md`); type files' settings are not applied.
- Every build ends with the frame probe: an id pass through the real camera, 8–12 frames. Declare what must read as `shot.key_parts` (with windows); open `frame_probe/*_id.png` in the version when a `FRAME_*` / `KEY_PART_*` code comes back. → `references/blender_freedom.md`
- The picture the user asked for is written as numbers in `shot.screen`, then built to them: where the subject or a part sits and how big (`targets`: `center_x/y`, `width/height_share`, `area_share`, `edge_margin`, `thirds_distance`, `ui_overlap`, `speed`, each `value ± tol` over frames), how fast it may cross the frame (`max_speed`, ~0.006 frame widths per frame keeps a move steady), what a generated take must keep as Blender pixels (`keep`), and - when no single built subject is the subject (a street, a section, a hall) - what is (`subject`: ids, group ids `st.slab` = `st.slab.0…`, project subjects placed by fill). Every declared id (key and concealed parts, `subject`, `keep`) is resolved by one rule - as written, the layout's spelling (`concrete_hall` builds as `concrete-hall`), fill copies (`fill-<id>`), groups - and one that matches nothing fails the build with the closest built ids; a shot with no subject at all warns `SUBJECT_UNDECLARED` (archcut3 s01, 2026-10-08: no subject, so every screen number measured nothing). Words → targets ("engine in the upper third, two thirds of the height" → `center_y 0.33 ±0.05`, `height_share 0.66 ±0.08`); a composition reference image → `reference view add` (without `--subject` for a scene view: world `xyz` points, solved in world space) + `reference_fit_camera` for the camera, then targets for what remains; the storyboard sheet is where the user sees it. The probe measures them on the same id pass (no render) and returns `screen` with each target's value and score in the build result. Why: until 2026-10-07 the frame was judged only on minimums (not empty, not cut, not tiny), so "what the user wanted" lived in no value and no check. A miss is a warning to read, not a rebuild to silence; a declared key part the platform UI covers wherever it shows (vertical output: top 14 %, bottom 35 %, sides 6–11 %) is an error (`KEY_PART_UNDER_UI`). → `references/storyboard.md`
- Unknown or version-sensitive bpy names: `api search --query` / `api show --path` (index of the installed Blender) before writing code.
- People are library characters, not modelled figures: `shot.scene.characters [{id, asset, action: walk|run|idle|work, path | at, speed_mps, height_m, phase, appear_frame, texture}]` - rigged CC0 people (`studio character register` adds one from an FBX with its licence; `character show`) that walk a path with no foot slide (the action plays at the speed the planted foot needs), at their real height, each copy out of step. Their look is what generation may restyle; size, pose, gait and position are Blender's. Why: archcut3's inspectors were boxes swung at the hip. A person who handles something declares it: `contacts [{limb: left_hand|right_hand|left_foot|right_foot, target: <object or part id> | [x,y,z], local?: [x,y,z] in the target's own coordinates (the pedal end of a crank turns with it), why}]` and `stance: stand|crouch` - the limbs follow their targets by IK (elbows back, knees forward), the body moves by the least distance (and turns up to 90 deg) to where it passes through nothing, every contact still held (`character_pose.json`). A body inside a solid thing stops the build (`CHARACTER_THROUGH_OBJECT`, `character_clearance.json`: character, object, frame, bone); a target out of reach says so (`CONTACT_UNREACHABLE`). Never pose hands by hand in an author script: nothing keeps that body out of the object. Why (2026-10-19): the bike mechanic's chest was 0.11 m inside the front wheel in six shots - he stood on the far side and reached across the bike. Bad: an author IK on the hand bones, the body where it was. Good: `contacts` on the shifter and the crank's pedal end, `at` on the drivetrain side, `stance: crouch`.
- What the narration explains must show, not only exist: list those items in `shot.screen.details` (`{id, min_px, why}`: visible thickness in output pixels - a layer edge-on across the frame is only as thick as it shows) - `DETAIL_NOT_SHOWN` until a frame in the window shows them that big. A detail too thin to read — in order: 1) a cut face toward the camera, 2) a closer camera or a longer lens, 3) a declared exaggeration: build it larger and say so in data (`display_scale {factor ≤4, reason}` on the scene row, or a subject dimension deviation with `factor`) plus a label anchored to it while it shows ("확대 표현 ×3") — the scale audit then judges real size × factor and the build refuses an unlabeled one (`DISPLAY_SCALE_UNDISCLOSED`). Forbidden: thickening a part without declaring it (the viewer takes it as the real size). Bad: a 3 cm buffer modelled 9 cm and nothing said. Good: `display_scale {factor: 3, reason: "30 mm buffer reads at phone size"}` + label "완충재 30 mm (확대 표현 ×3)". Give the structure a world: it carries on past the frame (floors, neighbours, surroundings: repeats, kits, a backdrop relation); a model ending in a void on three sides is `FRAME_MODEL_EDGE` unless `screen.diorama`; an interior lit by an HDRI hides it from the camera (`style.world.hide_from_camera`). Something spreading through a structure (vibration, heat, water, a crack) is the `propagation` shader on its surface (`shader: {kind: propagation, params: {origin, speed_mps, start_frame, band_m, fade_s}}`), not drawn lines. A reaction is `look_at` (the head turns to a point or object) and a one-off action is `playback: once` (a jump) on a library character. Why (floor_noise, 2026-10-09): every floor layer was in the scene at its true 3 cm and none was readable; the cutaway floated in a factory HDRI; the vibration was a few lines; the child's jump read as a walk.
- Layout is data in `shot.scene` (exemplars, kits, repeats, levels, section, binds). Subject shape is modelled any way - spec builders with `ops`, the casting / cage kits, or any bpy in the author script; numbers that make a claim come from sources, appearance numbers are illustrative. Every path is judged the same. → `references/declarative_scene.md`
- Create or patch through shot build. The shot's author script is `shot.author` (set once by the first build with `--script`, or by `set`): a fresh build runs it, a different `--script` is refused (`AUTHOR_SCRIPT_CONFLICT`) - change the field instead. Why: archcut3 s02/s03 were rebuilt without `--script` and lost their materials and gait. Author scripts receive a job JSON after `--`, containing `shot`. Custom animation sets scene['studio_authored_animation']=True; otherwise standard action/camera contracts apply. A declared `shot.camera.rig` is baked after the author script even when that flag is set. Use --base to patch saved geometry.
- Craft rules for geometry, disassembly, cuts, textures and reference studies: `references/blender_craft.md` (always read before authoring geometry or materials).
- Matter that falls or drifts (debris through an opening, dust): a `simulate` action bound to cues, baked into the version at build. Never physics evaluated at render time: workers render frames in any order, so the build refuses `SIMULATION_NOT_BAKED`. → `references/scatter_simulation.md`
- Sound: a music bed `project.audio.music {sound, gain_db, duck_ratio, fade_in_s, fade_out_s}` ducks under the narration (sidechain), and `shot.sfx [{id, sound, cue | frame, offset_frames, gain_db}]` puts effects on events (a cut opening, a tag appearing); sounds are library entries with their licence (`sound list`, `sound register` for CC0 / CC-BY files), named in sources.md. No library sound fits: `sound generate-review` (one sheet) and `sound generate` in the user's words. Why: the edit mixed the voice only, and silence reads as unfinished.
- Cuts: `shot.transition {kind: cut|dissolve|dip|whip, frames, color_srgb}` on the incoming shot; every kind keeps both shots' frame counts, so narration and subtitles do not move (speed shaping inside a shot is the camera timing's job).
- Subtitle look: `style.captions` (`size_frac`, `color_srgb`, `outline_srgb`, `outline_frac`, `panel: rounded|none`, `panel_srgb`); a tag on the thing itself (a red box with white text on a column): a label with `placement: anchor`, `fill_srgb`, `text_srgb` - it follows the projected anchor. `studio/edit.py` is not frozen; ask for a missing look instead of reporting it impossible.
- Arrows, dimension lines, outlines, drawn-on paths: `shot.graphics`, a separate layer (`graphics render`, composited in edit under labels). Forbidden: mesh arrows or 3D text for explanation — they get lit, bloomed and leak into control passes. → `references/explainer_graphics.md`
- Titles and place names: `shot.titles` (2D, receding or held, safe-rect checked every frame). → `references/titles.md`
- Look: set `render.look_preset` (or `style.look.preset`): `flat_stylized` (default, no-op), `photoreal_product|exterior|interior|night`, `previs_clay` (hybrid input only). The preset's light rig is a default: `render.lighting.rig` sets the key / fill / rim of this shot (direction relative to the camera, ratio to the key, softness, colour), and `screen.light` states the light the picture needs - the key's direction and stops, and `regions` (how bright each band cell or `class:<id>` should read, L*): the workbench `light_targets {frame}` measures them on a lit preview and solves the light-group multipliers (per-group contributions, `light_probe`), proposing rig values or naming the group to change. Read `references/look_photoreal.md` before choosing.

## Phase 3 — Review stills, then motion
- Prepare a whole-reel rough cut early. Prove the hardest hero frame from multiple angles at look quality before full renders. Compare reference, first attempt and latest at equal display size.
- Render and view images; correct materials, composition, occlusion and detail. Process success isn't visual acceptance. Preserve the best version when an iteration regresses.
- Previews are free and unlimited (workbench `preview` with `lit`, `reference_compare`); only counted renders are budgeted. A real subject (`specific_real`) is matched to licensed photos from two sides before its look renders (fidelity `photo coverage`): when none was supplied, collect them (`reference collect`, through `studio_run`; `references/subject_fidelity.md` 1b). With a reference photo: write its visible features as a checklist before modelling, match its camera (`reference view add` → `reference_fit_camera`), then iterate on `reference_compare` at that view until every item passes - spending counted renders on an unchecked exterior is the failure the engine test (2026-10-07) measured. Request template: `docs/REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`; procedure → `references/workbench.md` (Matching a reference photo).
- Passing is the floor, not the finish: while previews are free, run `reference_critique {image, frame}` (workbench; or `studio critique frames|video`, and `qa collect --reference` fills `visual_checks`) - the differences from the reference frame of the same moment, region by region (bands and our id classes: brightness, colour, contrast, detail), largest first, each with the shot value that would close it - and fix the largest difference; stop when no remaining difference shows in a crop or the request's appearance budget per shot (builds or minutes) is spent - then list the remaining differences with crops and go on to the next shot. The same budget covers chasing `screen` targets: a target still missed when it is spent is reported with its measured value, not rebuilt again.
- Keep going while the budget lasts: every build reports `appearance {used, budget, remaining}` (limits.appearance_iterations_per_shot, default 6). After each build a separate high-effort reviewer answers `references/visual_review.md` on the hero frame against the concept (`reference_critique` defaults to them); any fail with builds remaining → change the one thing it names, rebuild. Stop on all-pass or `APPEARANCE_BUDGET_SPENT`, then list what still differs with crops. Why: floor_noise passed every check with three quarters of its budget left and read as a doll's house.

## Phase 4 — Generative / hybrid shots (after approval only)
Order: `route.role` → `generate inputs` (role decides clay+depth or look render) → `prompt_spec` (look / keep / add / forbid) → look reference stills for reference models (`generate still`; never third-party frames) → `generate prompt` → `generate review` (show the sheet; the user adds or changes items in their words → edit `prompt_spec` → `generate review --after <id> --user-words "..."`) → `route approve --review <id> --user-words "..."` → `generate clip` → show the takes → `generate select --user-words "..." --additions present:<item>,absent:<item>`.
Who does what (2026-10-10, the user: "구조를 완벽하게 지키는것보다 봤을때 보기에 좋아야"): the generative model fills 100 % of a hybrid frame from the lit look render; structure is held by three things, cheapest first - 1) the declared key parts (`key_parts`, `screen.subject`, `screen.keep`, label anchors), 2) the prompt's structure clause (`generate prompt` measures the explained parts' screen order and thickness and says it: "layers from top to bottom: brown wood, about 10 %; grey mortar, about 50 %; …"), 3) after the take, `auto_keep` puts back from Blender pixels only the parts the take lost (`qa.parts`), declared keeps, labelled parts and decals, colour-matched at the seam (`SEAM_COLOUR_FAR` when the take relit the scene). Whole-frame edges are a warning on explain shots, not a gate. Bad: "s02 is explain, so `screen.keep: [floor, room]` - the model only gets the child" (the frame stays a Blender render). Good: "s02 `key_parts: [floor/eps]`, labels on wood and mortar; the model fills the frame; the take lost eps → eps put back, labels ride on the kept wood and mortar". Optional insurance for a reference model: `generate structure-still-review` (one paid edit of the look-render frame with the structure clause, the user's words) → `generate inputs --reference images/<id>/0.png`. Hybrid inputs: `generate inputs` sends the look render (every role); the control clay must exist as the QA baseline (`generate control --kinds depth,clay`, add `normal` for the light check). `shot.screen.generate_only [part ids, '@background']` still hands the model only those regions (people, the sky) when that is what the shot needs. Stills: `generate image-review --purpose texture|plate|edit`, a generated texture becomes a material only through `generate register-texture` in the user's words. Video-input models only (seedance-2.5, wan-2.2-vace, kling-o1-edit, luma-ray-modify); first-frame models (Veo i2v) invent motion and are refused for hybrid. Details and fallback order: `references/generative_safety.md`.
Frame rate: every take that is not 30 fps is made 30 fps by motion interpolation (`retime: interpolate`, RIFE; default), never by repeating frames - a 24 fps take duplicated stutters every fourth frame (`GENERATED_JUDDER` in the take's QA). → `references/generative_safety.md` (Run)

## Phase 5 — Edit, QA, delivery
- Make scratch narration, align actions and ensure speech fits; never truncate it silently. Resolve cue-bound actions before final render. Mark scratch voice explicitly.
- Rented GPU (RunPod) renders: `render submit --remote` runs the same worker on a server made for the queue and deleted the moment no remote job remains; off until `gpu enable --user-words "…" --monthly-cap N` in the user's words, refused past the monthly cap at the price the server is billed (`GPU_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`: a new server over it, or with no price, is deleted unused) and stopped when the month reaches it mid-render (`GPU_BUDGET_REACHED`: the estimate is a guess); `render quote --project P --shots s01:v0008,... --profile final` prices the whole queue on one server (cached shots free, one boot) before you submit, and a submit whose queue would pass the cap makes no job (`GPU_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`); `studio_run ["gpu", "status"]` lists the servers and spend (in your own CLI it answers from the ledger only: `REMOTE_GPU_LIVE_UNAVAILABLE`), `gpu sweep` deletes ours nobody holds. Ask before the first remote render of a session. Why: the Mac renders a 30 s final in about 2 hours and overloads; a server left running bills by the second.
- Submit renders, inspect job status, recover partial frames. Build edit and collect QA. Inspect contact sheets and full frames; encoding checks don't establish factual or visual correctness.
- Return the requested artifact: stills for a still study, playable output when video is requested, plus scene snapshots, concrete reference differences and the next valuable change. Do not render an animation merely to satisfy a default workflow when the current still-quality gate fails. Respect iteration budgets; change strategy on repeated failure.

## Starting points
| Item | Default | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Samples | look 64, final 128 (16-bit PNG) | animation ≥ 64 unless set explicitly |
| Real scale | within ±5 % of real dimensions | flag_ratio ≤ 0.2 |
| Bevel | machined 0.5–2 mm; cast / molded 3–10 mm (as the reference shows) | a declared guard-pair distance never changes |
| Lens (photoreal) | 50–85 mm product/mechanism | 18–28 mm only for high-energy rigs |
| Key:fill | 2–4 : 1 | no plain grey cyclorama for photoreal |
| Motion blur shutter | 0.5 | label anchors blur ≤ 2 px |
| Atmosphere (opt-in) | fog density 0.01–0.03 inside a `box`, beams 12–20° | never over a whole site (reads as haze) |
| Blockout preview | Workbench ~0.02 s/frame for blocking and motion; Cycles + Fast GI for lit review | EEVEE renders headless but its lighting does not match Cycles |

Revision invariants:
- Saved .blend snapshots are immutable. Create new versions and preserve unrelated projects. A revision starts from the base version's `authored.blend`, so it equals a fresh build with the same inputs.
- For each revision, explicitly list the constraints required by that request in top-level preserve. An explicit list replaces the previous list; omission inherits it. Replace a previous metadata-only scene_version constraint with geometry/materials when a camera change is requested. Do not clear unrelated user constraints merely to make a patch pass.
- Text-only changes reuse 3D; camera/geometry invalidate affected shots only. Pin asset versions.
- Frames are 0-based half-open; convert +1 at Blender. Preview/final share 30fps.
- Labels follow real anchors; hide missing/behind-camera coordinates and review moving callouts.
- Credentials remain in environment, never logs. Missing paid voice access permits scratch completion. Purchasing, posting and external messages need their own authorization.
- Candidate and human-approved master differ. Never manufacture human approval; progress to a candidate without repetitive intermediate permission requests.

Reporting: report gate results, the numbers that matter (costs, frame times, metrics) and what you see; report every failed or skipped check; never compress a failure into "mostly works".
