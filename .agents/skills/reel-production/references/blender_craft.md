# Blender craft rules (moved verbatim from SKILL.md on 2026-10-04)

Read when authoring or revising geometry, materials, textures or reference studies. These are field-tested corrections; do not paraphrase them away.

- Search local assets first, then use asset tools or research authorized sources. Inspect actual geometry and map stable part IDs; downloaded doesn't mean separable or closeup-ready.
- For fine disassembly, compare the provider's native .blend with glTF before splitting geometry. glTF seams can create many disconnected vertex islands that are not semantic parts. Keep the source pinned, separate a derived copy, preserve materials, map reviewed components, and judge closeup suitability at the intended screen size. Never infer a working internal mechanism from a detailed exterior.
- For a reference-quality study, select an exact reference time and write visible acceptance criteria before authoring. Compare the reference, first attempt and latest image at equal display size. Check attachment visibility and material scale, not just whether objects exist. The facade study in projects/harness_validation/hero_quality records successful corrections and remaining limits; it is not a reference-quality-approved recipe.
- Verify that authored slots/holes actually cut through the evaluated mesh (for example, a ray test), then inspect them from the render camera. A correct cut can still be covered by another part. Keep anchors visible when shortening/repositioning a foreground member.
- Inspect texture content before reuse: a metal filename may depict diamond tread or rust unsuitable for smooth sheet. Pin source/copy hashes, preserve source assets, pack required images, and keep color versus roughness/normal color spaces correct. Fine bump alone may disappear at output size; vary material color/roughness at a controlled scale and judge in the rendered image.
- If a texture remains stretched after changing source maps, measure evaluated face UV differentials against actual surface lengths before more lookdev. Primitive cube UV faces may be rotated and occupy only a quarter atlas. Author explicit physical-scale UVs and assert equal repeats per metre on the intended surface axes when isotropy is required; a Mapping-node scale alone is not proof. See hero_quality/inspect_wall_uv.py and its v0010/v0011 audit.
- For materials on parts that may move later, anchor procedural patterns to object-local coordinates (or an intentional stable asset-space reference). World Position can cause textures to slide across moving geometry; do not claim motion stability merely from a still.

## Blender API version
Blender 5.x moved or removed APIs that older material still teaches (e.g. `Mesh.use_auto_smooth` → `shade_smooth()` + `set_sharp_from_angle()`, node-group modifier inputs under `NodesModifier.properties`). Before writing a bpy call you have not used in this repo: `api search --query "..."` / `api show --path bpy.types.X.y` (built from the installed version), or `workbench call --tool api_lookup`. Forbidden: guessing a property name and wrapping it in try/except.
