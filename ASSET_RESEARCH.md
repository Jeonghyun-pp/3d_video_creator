## Listings excluded from purchase consideration

- [CGTrader “Cityscape Seoul South Korea”](https://www.cgtrader.com/3d-models/exterior/cityscape/cityscape-seoul-south-korea-c8d49f8e-5639-44a1-a36c-d983cc2cfbd6) and the similar [TurboSquid cityscape](https://www.turbosquid.com/3d-models/3d-model-cityscape-seoul-south-korea-2313895) mention DDP in generic Seoul description text. Neither page verifies that DDP geometry is included, recognizable, or detailed enough for a hero shot.
- A search of public Sketchfab and SketchUp 3D Warehouse listings did not identify a named, downloadable high-quality DDP building model. [Sketchfab's download documentation](https://sketchfab.com/developers/download-api) also requires account authentication; [SketchUp's 3D Warehouse guide](https://help.sketchup.com/ko-kr/3d-warehouse/getting-started-3d-warehouse) requires sign-in to download third-party models. This is a search result limit, not proof that no private or unindexed model exists.

**Current asset gate:** no candidate above is a verified, licensed, production-quality DDP exterior geometry package. The next actionable questions for the official holders are the shell mesh/BIM export format, panel-detail LOD, allowed derivative rendering and commercial/social-media use, attribution, and cost. No paid listing should be purchased on the evidence currently available.
